# Methodology

This supplementary report presents the tabular settlement gazetteer accompanying the regional demographic review of the Russian Pontic Steppe (10000BC - 1900AD). The dataset contains 176 individual settlements across the Lower Don, Azov Littoral, Taman Peninsula, and Kuban-Stavropol Steppe from 10000BC to 1900AD, indexing each settlement with alternate and historical names, decimal coordinates, and chronological population estimates for inhabited benchmark years.

Population figures are derived from excavated dwelling floor areas and settlement plans for prehistoric and Bronze Age sites [@belanovskaya1995rakushechnyy; @tsybrij2018rakushechnyy; @dolbunova2021rakovinnyye; @skorobogatov2025keramicheskiy; @potapov2013krest; @sharafutdinova1980kobyakovo], walled urban areas and rural surveys for Classical Bosporan, Maeotian and Sarmatian settlements [@paromov1992karta; @kuznetsov2010phanagoria; @garbuzov2011osvoyeniye; @garbuzov2013selskaya; @marchenko2000elizavetovskoye; @kopylov2016vremeni; @batieva2011naseleniye; @vdovchenkov2019meotov], fortress and urban excavations for Khazar Saltovo-Mayaki and Golden Horde cities [@artamonov1958sarkel; @afanasyev1993donskiye; @flyorov2011goroda; @maslovsky2017azak; @maslovsky2022tokhtamysh; @maslovsky2024chislennosti] and Russian *Posolsky Prikaz* rolls, Imperial tax revisions (*revizii*) and the 1897 Census for Don and Kuban Cossack *stanitsas*, peasant *slobodas* and cities [@mininkov1998donskoye; @sushchiy2019institut; @prudnikov2024gorodok; @pronshtein1961zemlya; @kabuzan1976zaseleniye; @agafonov2017zemli; @kolomeytseva2018krestyanstva; @burykina2008osvoyeniye; @parshina2023kubanskogo; @volvenko2023priazovya; @troynitsky1905don; @troynitsky1905kuban].
