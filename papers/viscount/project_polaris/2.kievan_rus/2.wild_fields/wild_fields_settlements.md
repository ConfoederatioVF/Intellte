# Gazetteer Methodology, Schema, and Five-Part Sub-Regional Scope

## Scope and Settlement Data Architecture

This companion settlement gazetteer documents 191 individual archaeological, proto-urban, fortified, and medieval urban settlements located across the ~238 000 km² domain of the Wild Fields (partitioned into five natural sub-regions: I: Budjak [Danube-Prut-Dniester Steppe and Liman], II: NW Pontic Steppe [Dniester-Bug Interfluve], III: Right-Bank Bug-Dnieper and Ingul-Ingulets, IV: Left-Bank Lower Dnieper, Veliky Lug, and Northern Taurida, and V: Middle Seversky Donets, Donets Ridge, and Azov Basin) between 10000BC and 1400AD.

Every population entry records a positive integer (> 0) anchored to archaeological site area and dwelling counts, classical *polis* and *chora* surveys, *Dridu* and *Saltovo-Mayaki* fortification enclosures, Kievan Rus chronicle and archaeological evidence, and Golden Horde, Genoese, and Moldavian port records up to 1400AD [@kryzhitsky1999; @subbotin2000; @kravchenko2020; @dovzhenok1961; @bocharov2015; @boguslavskiy2014]. Zero values are omitted; when a settlement is abandoned or destroyed (for example, Sredny Stog after 3500BC, Olbia after 375AD, Sidorovo after 965AD, or Kuchugury in 1395AD), its time series terminates cleanly at its final attested benchmark. All coordinates are verified in decimal degrees (45,32°N to 50,06°N, 28,29°E to 39,55°E), and all historical and alternate toponyms are transliterated into Latin script.
