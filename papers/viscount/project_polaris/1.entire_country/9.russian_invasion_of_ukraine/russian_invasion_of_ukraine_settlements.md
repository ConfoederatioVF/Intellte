# Methodology

This settlement gazetteer documents annual resident population trajectories from 2021AD to 2026AD for 245 cities, frontline towns, regional administrative seats, military garrisons, hydrocarbon boomtowns, and defence-industrial centres across the 123 administrative and occupation-history divisions of the war theatre (Table \ref{tab:gaz-summary}). Coverage spans all 33 Ukrainian oblast and occupation-history divisions across Ukraine's 27 constitutional oblasts, special-status cities, and Autonomous Republic of Crimea, alongside all 7 administrative divisions of Belarus and all 83 federal subjects of the Russian Federation [@libanova2024demographic; @iom2025returns; @belstat2024chislennost; @rosstat2024demografiya; @mediazona2025casualties].

Each entry records geographic coordinates, historical and alternative toponyms, sovereign country attribution, and annual headcounts across every benchmark year (2021AD, 2022AD, 2024AD, and 2026AD in the summary tables below), cross-checked against ACLED raion conflict intensity, DeepState frontline polygons, Copernicus Sentinel-1 radar and UNOSAT optical damage assessments, and municipal internal displacement registers [@acled2025ukraine; @deepstate2025map; @scher2023sar; @unosat2023mariupol]. Every one of the 123 analytical divisions is represented by at least its administrative seat alongside major frontline or industrial centres.
