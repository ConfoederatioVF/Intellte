# Gazetteer Structure and 38-Region Coverage (1400AD - 1721AD)

This settlement gazetteer accompanies the demographic literature review for the Tsardom of Russia, Baltic Provinces, Lithuanian-Polish Rus, and Siberian Colonisation (1400AD - 1721AD) and compiles 205 individual georeferenced settlements across all 38 period-authentic regions spanning Russia and Siberia (109 settlements across Regions 18-38), Ukraine and Crimea (47 settlements across Regions 9-17), Estland-Livland, Courland-Inflanty, Core Lithuania, and Old Finland (27 settlements across Regions 1-4), and Belarus (22 settlements across Regions 5-8) [@Vodarsky1973; @Vodarsky1977; @Kabuzan1963; @Palli1996; @Dunsdorfs1962; @Kiaupa2000; @KarpachevKozlovsky1972; @Yakovenko2006; @Dolgikh1960]. Every Hanseatic port, Magdeburg-law town (*miasteczko*), ducal residence, Swedish border fortress, Cossack regimental capital, southern abatis-line stronghold, Ural salt or ironworks settlement, and Siberian *ostrog* is recorded as an independent entry with decimal-degree coordinates (`[lat, lon]`) and positive integer population benchmarks across its documented period of occupation between 1400AD and 1720AD [@Tikhomirov1962; @Bernotas2013; @Sharukho2010; @Rezun1989; @Zhigalov2024].

Population series are derived from Baltic *Hakenrevisionen* and municipal burger rolls, the 1528AD Lithuanian *Popis* and *Voloka* / *podymne* hearth registers, Muscovite land cadastres (*pistsovye knigi*), the 1646AD and 1678AD Household Censuses (*perepisnye knigi*), the First Poll-Tax Revision (1719-1721AD, *revizskie skazki*), Left-Bank Ukrainian *perepysni knyhy* (1666AD) and regimental *komputy*, Ottoman *tahrir defterleri* for Crimea and Kefe, and Siberian *yasak* and *dozornye* books [@Shapiro1974; @Morzy1965; @Vasiliauskas2012; @Saloheimo1976; @Irtenina2022; @Ozturk2019; @Pokrovsky1984]. Zero-population placeholders are omitted so that each settlement's benchmark keys reflect its active historical span within the 1400AD - 1721AD domain.
