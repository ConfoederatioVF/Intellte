# Methodology

This supplementary settlement gazetteer accompanies the regional demographic literature review for the post-Stalin Soviet Union (1953AD - 1991AD) and compiles 292 individual urban, industrial, scientific, naval, resource-frontier, and evacuated settlements across all forty-one Late-Soviet regions of the Estonian SSR (Region 1), Latvian SSR (Region 2), Lithuanian SSR (Region 3), Moldavian SSR (Region 4), Kaliningrad Oblast RSFSR exclave (Region 5), Byelorussian SSR (Regions 6-8), Ukrainian SSR (Regions 9-15), and mainland RSFSR (Regions 16-41) [@tssu_1962_perepis_1959; @tssu_1972_perepis_1970; @tssu_1984_perepis_1979; @goskomstat_1991_perepis_1989; @goskomstat_1992_demograficheskiy_ezhegodnik_1991]. Every entry records geographic coordinates, historical Soviet or pre-Soviet toponyms and functional designations, modern republic attribution (Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Moldova, Belarus, Ukraine, or Russia), and positive integer population series across the six post-Stalin benchmark years (1953AD, 1959AD, 1970AD, 1979AD, 1989AD, and 1991AD, plus 1986AD pre-evacuation benchmarks for settlements inside the Chernobyl Exclusion Zone) [@lappo_1997_geografiya_gorodov; @rowland_1996_russia_secret_cities_zato; @iaea_2006_chernobyl_environmental_consequences].

To facilitate regional cross-referencing across all forty-one Late-Soviet regions, Section 2 tabulates the 47 enumerated settlements of the Baltic SSRs (Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania), the Moldavian SSR, and the Kaliningrad Oblast Baltic Fleet exclave (Regions 1-5, including Snieckus/Visaginas, Sillamae, Tiraspol, Ribnita, Comrat, and Baltiysk), Section 3 tabulates the 82 settlements of the Byelorussian SSR (Regions 6-8) and Ukrainian SSR (Regions 9-15, including the 1954AD Crimean transfer and the 1986AD Chernobyl Exclusion Zone), Section 4 tabulates 107 settlements across European Russia, Leningrad-Novgorod-Pskov (Region 17), the Karelian and Komi ASSRs, the *Nechernozemye* depopulation ring, the Volga-Kama and Ural ASSRs, the 1957AD restored North Caucasus and Kalmyk ASSRs, and declassified *ZATO* nuclear cities (Regions 16-30), Section 5 tabulates 56 settlements across the Yamalo-Nenets and Khanty-Mansi hydrocarbon okrugs, Western Siberia, the Angara-Yenisei hydro cascades, the Tuva, Buryat, and Yakut ASSRs, the Baikal-Amur Mainline (BAM), and the Russian Far East (Regions 31-41), and Section 6 provides the complete unified alphabetical directory across all forty-one regions.
