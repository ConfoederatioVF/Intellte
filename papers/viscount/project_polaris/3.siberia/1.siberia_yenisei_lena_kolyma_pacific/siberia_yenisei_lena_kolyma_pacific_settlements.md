# Introduction

This supplementary report presents the tabular settlement gazetteer accompanying the regional demographic review of Eastern and Pacific Siberia (10000BC - 1900AD; Figure \ref{fig:hero}). The regional dataset follows the flat Stadester structure, indexing 281 individual settlements by name and country with six standardised fields (name, alternate names, coordinates, country, key, and population) sorted in chronological order (Tables \ref{tab:ii1} to \ref{tab:iv3}). Active occupation spans correspond directly to the first and last recorded benchmark years in each settlement's population history.

Prehistoric camp and pit-house village figures are derived from excavated dwelling counts, floor areas, and cemetery sizes [@Chernykh2022; @Weber2016a; @Kuzmin2006; @AlekseyevDyakonov2009; @Fitzhugh2016; @Dneprovskiy2017]. Estimates for Iron Age and medieval fortified settlements (*gorodishcha*) in Transbaikalia, Tuva, Khakassia, the Amur Basin, and Primorye are drawn from intramural areas and excavated house-pit totals [@Vasyutin2022; @Tulush2011; @Tulush2016; @Artemyeva2013; @Artemyeva2025; @Gelman2018; @Ivliev2021]. Early Russian fortresses (*ostrogi*), 18th-century mining and border towns, and 19th-century urban centres are compiled from *yasak* and garrison rolls [@Dolgikh1960; @Chertykov2009; @Ushnitskiy2017a; @Turaev2013], imperial revision lists and historical urban studies [@Kabuzan1985; @Mansheev2010; @Kurchatova2015; @Kyshpanakov2022a; @Kolomiets2022; @Rygalova2024], and the 1897 First All-Russian Imperial Census [@Troinitskiy1905; @Patkanov1912].
